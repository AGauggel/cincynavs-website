Who we are

What if one day a Coke vending machine falls over and traps you under it? (True story!) You better hope there's someone close by to help get you out alive. We need people by our side to get through life, to help us grow, to teach us, and to challenge us.

That's the whole idea behind Cincy Navs — a community of UC students building deep friendships where we can be known and loved, digging into Scripture together, walking with each other through whatever college actually throws at us. All with the goal of looking more like Jesus, sharing our faith, and helping others do the same.

Our history

Cincy Navs has been active on campus since 1989 — over three decades of UC students building the kind of friendships that outlast a semester.

What we believe

We hold to The Navigators' Statement of Faith, Calling, and Core Values — the same beliefs held by Navigators ministries on campuses and in communities worldwide.

A campus ministry of The Navigators

Cincy Navs is a local branch of The Navigators, an international, interdenominational Christian ministry founded in 1933. We're one small part of a much larger, established family of ministries — on campuses, in cities, in workplaces, and around the world.
