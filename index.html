Friendship, for the sake of the Gospel.

Cincy Navs is a community of UC students building deep friendships where we can be known and loved. We believe that Jesus changes lives (he's changed ours!), and that anyone can study the Bible. Let's dig deeper together, questions welcome.

We're a campus branch of The Navigators, meeting weekly at UC.

Who we are

The Navigators at UC are an outpost of the church scattered strategically among the lost for the sake of the Gospel. We're convinced that life to life discipleship changes lives and we want to invest in you and teach you how to invest your life in others.
